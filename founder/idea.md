# MOV — Social All-Sport Fitness & Nutrition App

- **What it is:** The "Strava meets MyFitnessPal + Hevy" for modern athletes. A unified mobile & web app that blends strength workouts, multi-sport activity tracking (padel, tennis, swimming, running, soccer), natural-language meal/nutrition logging, and a Strava-style social network with RPG fitness titles and leaderboards.
- **Who it is for:** Gym lifters, runners, and multi-sport athletes (padel/tennis/soccer/swimming) who want one social home for all their movement, conditioning, and nutrition, with friends and team leaderboards.
- **Core Product Features:**
  1. **All-Activity & Calorie Engine:** Log weightlifting or 30+ cardio/field sports (swimming, padel, tennis, running, soccer) with dynamic calorie burn calculated from METs, weight, height, and duration.
  2. **1-Click CSV Import:** Seamlessly migrate past workout logs from Strong, Hevy, or custom CSV so lifters never lose historical data.
  3. **Strava-Style Social Layer:** Activity feed, friend profiles, workout sharing, cheer/kudos, and team leaderboards.
  4. **RPG Athletic Attributes & Titles:** Earn XP in specific stats (Strength, Agility, Endurance) based on your routine. Playing soccer/padel levels up your **Agility** points and unlocks athlete titles (e.g., 'Playmaker', 'Vanguard', 'Iron Striker').
- **Business Model & Pricing:**
  - **Free Tier (100% Free):** Unlimited workout & multi-sport logging, friend feed, CSV import, basic rankings, and permanent cloud database sync. Free tier includes a generous weekly quota of AI meal analyses (e.g., 5/week, unlimited manual/barcode).
  - **Viral Growth Loop:** Invite 5 friends to MOV -> Unlock 1 Month of MOV Pro for free!
  - **MOV Pro ($4.99/mo or $29.99/yr with 14-day trial):** Unlimited AI meal parsing & voice breakdown, Adaptive AI Coach (auto-periodization, plate calculations, fatigue autoregulation, and dynamic calorie/macro adjustment based on sport burn), plus advanced stat analytics.
- **Distribution & Tech:** iOS, Android, and Web; centralized cloud database with user social graph and referral tracking.
