# MOV — Board Brief (Strategic Update: Social Growth Loop & Multi-Sport)

The founder has introduced major strategic pillars based on previous feedback:

1. **Eliminating Switching Friction:**
   - 1-Click CSV Import: Users can immediately import past logs and PR histories from Strong, Hevy, or custom spreadsheets.

2. **Strava-Style Social Network & Network Effects:**
   - Social feed, friend following, activity sharing, and community leaderboards.
   - **Viral Referral Loop:** "Invite 5 friends -> Get 1 Month of MOV Pro for free". This acts as the organic customer acquisition engine, drastically cutting CAC.

3. **Multi-Sport Activity Tracking & Dynamic Calorie Engine:**
   - Tracks sports beyond lifting: tennis, padel, swimming, running, soccer, basketball, cycling.
   - Calculates real calorie burn based on user biometric data (weight, height, sex) and exercise duration/METs.

4. **Gamification & Athletic Stat Progression (Agility / Strength / Endurance):**
   - Users earn attribute XP and dynamic titles tailored to how they move (e.g. playing soccer builds Agility XP and awards titles like 'Playmaker'; lifting builds Strength XP and awards 'Iron Vanguard').

5. **Freemium Guardrails & Monetization Tier:**
   - Free tier includes full core logging, social feed, CSV import, cloud sync, and 5 AI meal scans/week.
   - MOV Pro ($4.99/mo or $29.99/yr, 14-day trial) offers unlimited AI meal/voice parsing, the Adaptive AI Coach (auto-overload and dynamic nutrition balancing), and deep athletic stats.

Board members: Re-evaluate MOV through your respective published frameworks (Offers, Monopoly, Product) with these new network effects, viral loops, multi-sport TAM expansion, and switching-friction solutions.
